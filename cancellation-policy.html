Cancellation Policy

Please review our guidelines for cancelling or rescheduling an appointment.

24+

24 hours' notice or more

Cancellations made with at least 24 hours' notice before a scheduled appointment (via email, text, or phone call) will be processed without penalty.

!

Late cancellations & no-shows

Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice or client no-shows will be subject to a charge of the full service price. This includes situations where our team cannot access the property, is turned away, or the client is absent.

To avoid a cancellation fee, please provide at least 24 hours' notice before your appointment.

Need to cancel or reschedule?

You may cancel or reschedule by emailing us, texting, or calling our office.
