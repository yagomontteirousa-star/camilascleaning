Privacy Policy

How Camila's Cleaning uses information you choose to share when contacting us.

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Information you share

When you request a consultation or contact us, you may provide contact details, city, home size, rooms, service and frequency preferences, desired start date, referral source, and a message.

How we use information

We use this information to respond, discuss your needs, confirm service-area availability, prepare for a consultation, and communicate about requested services.

How the website form works

The consultation form prepares an email in your device's email application. You can review the message before sending it. The website does not create an account or store the completed form in a website database.

Third-party services and links

Phone, email, social platforms, and Google may process information under their own privacy terms. Camila's Cleaning does not control those external services.

Your choices

You may choose which optional details to include in a consultation request. You may also contact us to ask about information you previously provided or to request a correction.

Contact

Questions about this notice may be sent to info.camilascleaning@gmail.com or discussed by phone at (617) 909-3490.
