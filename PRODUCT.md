# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Homeowners and households in Greater Boston looking for dependable, personalized residential cleaning and ongoing care for their home. Secondary audiences include clients seeking office cleaning, post-construction cleaning, and move-in or move-out service.

## Product Purpose

Camila's Cleaning Inc helps clients return to a carefully maintained home and feel confident that their space, surfaces, preferences, and privacy will be treated with respect. The website's primary conversion is a request for a personal consultation; phone, text, and email remain direct alternatives.

## Positioning

A locally owned Greater Boston cleaning company positioned around attentive, consistent, relationship-led home care rather than discounts, urgency, or price competition.

## Operating Context

Prospective clients evaluate the company through its residential services, real photographs of completed work, Camila's founder story, existing client reviews, service area, consultation details, and direct contact channels.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing stack: static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- Existing contact channels: phone and SMS at +1 (617) 909-3490 and email at info.camilascleaning@gmail.com.
- Existing hours: Monday–Friday, 7:00 AM–6:00 PM; Saturday, 7:00 AM–3:00 PM; Sunday closed.
- Existing service area language: Greater Boston and Massachusetts Region.
- Residential services lead the home page. Office Cleaning and Post-Construction remain available with lower visual priority.
- The repository has no server-side form endpoint or third-party lead service. Consultation details may be validated in the browser and passed to the existing email channel without introducing an unapproved processor.
- Do not claim insurance status, certifications, guarantees, employee screening practices, key policies, team-assignment policies, specific cities, prices, or technology that the client has not confirmed.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the Camila's Cleaning name, logo, brand marks, authentic photography, contact information, hours, social profiles, and real client testimonials.
- Voice: calm, discreet, precise, warm, and confident; communicate trust, consistency, privacy, personalization, and care.
- Avoid promotional urgency, discount language, repeated “free quote” language, clichés centered on cleaning tools, and generic template aesthetics.
- Binding visual direction from the client brief: sophisticated editorial composition, warm neutral foundation, discreet olive/taupe accents, elegant display typography, highly legible body copy, generous whitespace, large interior photography, restrained icons, and subtle motion.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with residential home care and the feeling of returning to a well-cared-for home.
2. Earn trust with real people, real spaces, real reviews, and restrained factual language.
3. Make requesting a consultation calm, clear, and low-friction across form, phone, text, and email.
4. Preserve every confirmed contact and policy detail while labeling unconfirmed business facts as open decisions.
5. Keep the experience fast, accessible, responsive, and maintainable in the existing static stack.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus states, labeled and validated form controls, adequate color contrast, comfortable reading sizes, reduced-motion support, and touch targets suitable for mobile use.
