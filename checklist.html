Regular Residential CleaningWeekly and biweekly home care

  • Wipe mirrors
  • Dust blinds

  • Empty trash
  • Remove cobwebs
  • Dust picture frames and artwork
  • Wipe baseboards
  • Clean inside windows

Bathrooms

  • Scrub, wash, and sanitize toilets and sinks
  • Scrub, wash, and sanitize tubs and showers
  • Dust and wipe cabinet exteriors
  • Dust doors
  • Dust and wipe mirrors
  • Dust inside windows
  • Dust baseboards

Kitchen

  • Scrub and sanitize sink
  • Wipe cabinet faces
  • Clean microwave inside and outside
  • Scrub, clean, and sanitize countertops
  • Vacuum and mop floors
  • Dust and wipe all reachable surfaces
  • Wipe and clean inside refrigerator when reachable

  • Scrub and clean stovetop

Living and dining rooms

  • Dust all furniture
  • Vacuum sofa when possible

  • Hand wash doors
  • Clean basement upon request

Regular Commercial CleaningOffice and shared workplace care

Office areas

  • Vacuum and mop all floors
  • Dust all reachable surfaces, including desks, shelves, and electronics
  • Disinfect desks, phones, and keyboards
  • Empty trash and replace liners
  • Wipe doors, handles, and light switches

Common areas

  • Vacuum and mop all floors
  • Dust all furniture and surfaces
  • Clean and disinfect lobby and reception desk
  • Clean and polish glass doors and windows
  • Empty trash and replace liners

Break room / kitchenette

  • Vacuum and mop all floors
  • Wipe and disinfect tables and chairs
  • Clean countertops and sinks
  • Clean cabinet exteriors and handles
  • Wipe appliance exteriors, including microwave, fridge, and coffee machine
  • Remove trash and replace liners

Bathrooms

  • Vacuum and mop all floors
  • Clean and disinfect toilets, urinals, and sinks
  • Polish mirrors and chrome fixtures
  • Refill soap, paper towels, and toilet paper
  • Wipe walls around sinks and hand dryers
  • Empty trash and replace liners

Extra / janitorial services

  • Dust blinds and vents
  • Spot clean walls and doors
  • High dust ceiling vents, fans, and light fixtures
  • Restock supplies in kitchen and bathroom
  • Sweep and maintain building entrances and hallways
  • Clean elevator interiors and buttons
